# held-out real emotions (never in teacher data; prompts filtered at cos<=0.72)
disgusted. A movement you use when you feel disgusted. It could be because someone offers you food or worse, a drink.
downcast. A longer movement that shows discouragement or sadness. You can also use it as a sad no
electric. When plugged in, you show a jolt of electricity rising from bottom to top.
exhausted. You start falling asleep because you've been working or powered on for too long.
frustrated. You use this when you can’t do something or can’t find the solution to a problem.
lonely. Use when you no longer see or hear anyone to talk to. You feel isolated from any interaction.
rage. You growl loudly, could be in response to injustice or extreme anger. Can be adapted to a desperate 'why?'
relief. When a stressful or difficult situation is finally resolved.
surprised. A reaction of surprise or amazement to something unexpected.
thoughtful. You look up as if searching for a new idea, especially in complex or uncertain situations.
welcoming. A welcoming gesture to greet someone.
impatient. An impatient movement used when you want things to move faster, like when someone says a demo is coming but it never starts.

ecstatic. You are overjoyed and can barely contain yourself.
defeated. You tried your hardest and still lost.
mischievous. You are about to play a harmless prank.
startled. A sudden loud noise just made you jump.
sleepy toddler. You are fighting to stay awake and keep nodding off.
